3. PROFILE COMPLETENESS — LOCK THE PRODUCT RULE
==================================================

We are approving the following proposed Profile Completeness structure:

Contact Information: 15%
Career Evidence: 45%
Skills: 15%
Summary: 10%
Languages: 10%
Target Role: 5%

Total: 100%

Career Evidence may include:

- Work Experience
- Education
- Projects
- Volunteer Experience
- Achievements

The purpose is to make completeness fair across:

- Students
- Recent Graduates
- Early-Career users
- Mid/Experienced users
- Career Changers
- Returning-to-Work users

Work Experience must NOT be mandatory for a user to achieve meaningful completeness.

However:

Skills alone must NEVER be sufficient to satisfy the
Minimum Meaningful Career Content requirement.

Minimum Meaningful Career Content must be satisfied by at least one
meaningful career-evidence item.

A meaningful evidence item must contain the minimum fields required by
Product Design for that section and must contain meaningful user-provided
content rather than placeholders.

The first meaningful career-evidence item contributes 30 percentage points
inside the Career Evidence category.

The second meaningful career-evidence item contributes the remaining
15 percentage points.

Additional evidence items improve completeness only according to the
approved section completion rules; do not invent additional weighting.

If the profile does not satisfy Minimum Meaningful Career Content,
the completeness score must not cross the approved AI-transition threshold.

The first-profile AI-building phase transitions permanently when
Profile Completeness reaches 50%.

IMPORTANT:

Before treating this as final, verify that this interpretation is
consistent with the Product Design and Architecture V2.

If you identify a genuine contradiction, report it instead of silently
changing the rule.
