# 12 — Do not casually change

- Preserve Architecture V2, the Constitution and approved Product Design.
- Preserve native Flutter/Riverpod/Drift-SQLCipher/Supabase—not web/Expo replacement.
- Preserve confirmed Auth, secure recovery/callback identity and startup policy.
- Profile #1 provisioning must remain server-owned, exactly once and initially
  empty. Research answers are not fabricated CV facts.
- Career Profile is the editable source; Resume is configuration/rendering.
  Template/language changes do not create extra Profiles or translate content.
- Preserve RLS and owner-scoped commands. Never put service-role/provider keys,
  private keys, OAuth credentials or server API secrets in Flutter.
- Credits, allowances, entitlements and successful usage remain server-owned.
  No fake counters/ATS scores/PRO state or client balance authority.
- AI must preserve facts, ask for missing facts, propose before acceptance,
  preserve original/history, enforce versions, and use the approved model/global
  configuration. No silent model fallback.
- Preserve encrypted drafts, frozen retry identity, conflict prompts and
  account cleanup. No silent data deletion or overwrite.
- Preserve install completion independently of account-owned research answers.
  Existing-account sign-in cannot receive a fresh-install context overwrite.
- Arabic/English are first-class; IBM Plex Sans Arabic and real RTL apply.
- Preview/PDF share a canonical representation; no screenshot-based PDF, forced
  one-page clipping or content-dropping/font-shrinking workaround.
- Do not repurpose private PDF-only GCS into public photo hosting.
- Do not implement deferred Cover Letter, Professional Email, billing or
  advanced paid features while performing handoff.
- Preserve Git and historical documents. No force push, destructive squash,
  branch/tag deletion, secret commits or unsupported history claims.
