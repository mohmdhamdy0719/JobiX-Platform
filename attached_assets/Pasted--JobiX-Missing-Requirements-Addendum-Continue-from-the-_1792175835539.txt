# JobiX — Missing Requirements Addendum

Continue from the previously approved UI/UX refinement prompt. Treat both prompts as one combined specification. The following requirements were missing or insufficiently detailed and must also be addressed.

Do not repeat completed work unnecessarily. Inspect the current implementation first, preserve the successful Refresh and Dialog fixes, and fix the actual behavior rather than only hiding UI elements.

### 1. Cloud-first persistence and recovery

- Supabase remains the authoritative source for saved profile data.
- Local recovery is temporary protection for interrupted edits, not a second profile or an offline mode.
- Recover interrupted edits without silently treating them as saved.
- Allow the user to explicitly save recovered changes.
- Deliberately discarded changes must never be saved or restored later.
- Leaving an editor with unsaved changes must offer clear, safe choices.
- Remove internal UI wording such as “local draft,” “cloud version,” “review latest saved version,” and “back — retain local draft,” without removing the underlying recovery safeguards.
- Preserve authentication, RLS, validation, ownership checks, and existing data.

### 2. Onboarding and AI assistance

- Update the career-preferences explanation to:
  “Your preferences help us tailor your future JobiX experience. You can change them anytime in Settings.”
- Keep these preferences separate from resume content and preserve the existing answer options.
- Keep “Improve with JobiX” only where it has a clear purpose. Like remove it from unuseful places like Work Experience "Step 3 OF 6" Page&#x20;
- Remove misleading AI-generation indicators and irrelevant profile-completeness explanations from profile forms.

### 3. Guided questions across profile sections

In addition to the Professional Summary questions already specified, add compact, readable guidance for these sections:

**Work Experience**

- What was your role and what were you responsible for? Example: “Managed customer accounts and coordinated daily client requests.”
- What were your main contributions? Example: “Improved the team's reporting process and reduced repetitive manual work.”
- What measurable results did you achieve? Example: “Reduced report preparation time by 30%.”

**Education**

- What qualification did you earn, and in which field? Example: “Bachelor's degree in Business Administration.”
- Was there a relevant project, specialization, or academic achievement? Example: “Completed a research project on customer behavior and market segmentation.”

**Achievements**

- What did you accomplish? Example: “Received an award for outstanding project delivery.”
- Why was it important, or what was the result? Example: “Recognized for completing a cross-functional project ahead of schedule.”
- Is there a public link or evidence worth including? Example: a certificate, publication, award page, or portfolio URL.

**Volunteer Experience**

- What organization or cause did you support, and what was your role? Example: “Volunteered with a local community initiative to coordinate donation drives.”
- What did you contribute or accomplish? Example: “Helped organize events and coordinate volunteers.”

These are optional guidance prompts, not mandatory new fields. Examples are fictional and must never be inserted into actual user content automatically.
