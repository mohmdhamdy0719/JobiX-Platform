# JobiX — Fix Remaining UI/UX Issues and Build a New Development APK

The latest Development APK fixed the refresh behavior and dialogs successfully, but many previously requested UI/UX issues remain unresolved.

**Your task is to inspect the current implementation, fix the remaining issues below, preserve the successful fixes, and produce one fresh standard Development ARM64 debug APK.**

### Required fixes

1. **Welcome and Login**

   - Center the logo, content, and buttons.
   - Remove the redundant header logo.
   - Use this supporting text: `Build your career profile and prepare for your next opportunity.`

2. **Home and Profile Journey**

   - Remove the redundant sign-out icon from Home.
   - Continue Profile must resume the incomplete profile-building journey.
   - After profile completion, Continue Profile must lead to Career.
   - Persist completion correctly across logout and login.

3. **Professional Summary**

   - Redesign the description field to look professional and provide clear guidance.
   - Display these questions and examples separately from the actual resume content:
     - What is your professional background? Example: Marketing specialist with 3 years of experience in digital campaigns and content creation.
     - What are your strongest skills or areas of expertise? Example: SEO, campaign analytics, content planning, and stakeholder communication.
     - What kind of work or problems do you solve best? Example: Turning performance data into actionable campaign improvements.
     - What achievements or results are you most proud of? Example: Increased organic traffic by 25% over 6 months.
     - What role are you targeting next? Example: A digital marketing role focused on growth and experimentation.
     - What value would you bring to an employer or team? Example: Combining analytical thinking with clear communication to improve team outcomes.
   - Examples are illustrative only. Never insert them into the user's resume automatically or treat them as real user facts.

4. **Other Resume Sections**

   - Work Experience: guide users to describe responsibilities, key contributions, and measurable results.
   - Education: `Add relevant coursework, projects, honors, or academic achievements.`
   - Achievements: `Describe what you achieved, why it mattered, and any measurable result.`
   - Volunteer Experience: `Describe the cause you supported, your role, and how you contributed.`
   - Work Experience description: `Describe your responsibilities, key contributions, and measurable results.`
   - Remove redundant `Optional` labels where fields are not required, and remove unnecessary character-count guidance. Preserve actual validation.

5. **Language and Personal Information**

   - App language selector should appear only in onboarding and Settings.
   - Resume language selector belongs in Personal Information.
   - Make Arabic and English options equal in size and ensure correct RTL/LTR behavior.
   - Align Country and City input dimensions.

6. **Settings and Supporting Screens**

   - Remove redundant app-language controls from other screens.
   - Improve Photo and Custom Links copy and layout while preserving existing validation.
   - Remove obsolete local-draft/review-saved-version UI, but preserve safe data recovery.
   - Improve Settings copy and sign-out confirmation.
   - Do not invent account, support, privacy, or purchase functionality that does not exist.
   - Improve the ATS Professional template preview using clearly fictional, realistic sample data.

7. **Credits, Export History, and PDF Preview**

   - Preserve the successful refresh and dialog fixes from the previous build.
   - Keep Credit and export status server-authoritative.
   - Remove redundant manual refresh controls and repeated unnecessary polling.
   - Keep Download separate from loading states and preserve free downloads for eligible existing PDFs.
   - Display friendly template names instead of internal IDs.
   - Improve PDF spacing, page breaks, section cohesion, and Arabic/English RTL/LTR rendering.
   - Keep new Resume PDF export cost at 2 Credits and preserve existing accounting and idempotency safeguards.
   - Never run a live export or debit Credits during automated testing.
